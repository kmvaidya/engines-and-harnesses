### 2.3 Caveats the publisher states [P]

- "All counts are floors." Private repos, squash merges, disabled attribution and signing defaults hide work.
- "Units differ by agent (Codex is counted in pull requests, Replit in its per-prompt checkpoint commits), so read this as a concentration signal, not a like-for-like ranking."
- Declared-attribution estimates: "at the multi-million scale they can swing 30 to 40 percent between measurement runs." [P, methodology page]. [R] Earlier draft quoted "can swing 30 to 40 percent between runs at multi-million scale. Relative ranks are more robust than absolute magnitudes." as verbatim; the wording differs and the second sentence was not re-found in verification. Paraphrase it, do not quote.
- Claude Code's PR figure "is the softest in the table" (PR-template text match). Codex's count is inferred; an earlier signal was retired in Nov 2025.
- Copilot and Devin are sold mostly into private enterprise repos and are understated. Local Codex CLI work runs under the developer's identity and is largely invisible.
- Merge rates "include solo developers merging their own work, so they measure landed work, not independent review."
- Validation: random-sample manual checks; same attribution families as AgentPack (arXiv:2509.21891); proposes 0.25% to 2% false positives from Robbes et al. (arXiv:2601.18341) as its error band. Exact capture queries are kept internal [P].

### 2.4 Our reading [O]

- Per-agent review samples are not published. With 268 PRs across at least eight agents, several rates rest on a few dozen PRs or fewer (12.5% is consistent with 1 in 8). Present the review rates as "indicative" and quote the 44% overall.
- Claude Code's 83% partly reflects that it writes a co-author line into commits by default, which makes its work visible, while Copilot's in-editor and private-repo work is not. The 83% measures visible public output, not market share.
- Copilot's high review rate fits a product that opens a PR for a human to review, but Amplifying does not explain the gap and we did not verify a mechanism.
- Revenue per unit on the page (Devin $1,205, Cursor $276, Replit $112, Claude Code $14) divides run-rate by visible output; the page itself says it reflects "how much work is visible, not efficiency" [P]. Do not put it on a slide.

### 4.2 JetBrains State of Developer Ecosystem 2026
